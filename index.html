How Reconditioning Works

What happens between collecting a used drum and delivering it back.

Clean & Prepare

Interior and exterior washing, drying and surface preparation. Steel drums are shot blasted or washed depending on the previous contents; HDPE drums are hot washed.

Inspect & Test

Visual inspection for dents, corrosion and thread damage, then a leak test. Bungs, gaskets, lids and clamp rings are replaced where they will not seal.

Paint, Schedule & Deliver

Steel drums are repainted where required, then palletised and dispatched to your site on an agreed date anywhere in the UAE.

Steel Drums, Plastic Drums & IBC Tanks

New and reconditioned, held in stock at our Sharjah yard.

Steel Drums

Mild steel drums for solvents, lubricants, paints, fuels and hazardous goods, supplied new or reconditioned. Reconditioned drums are washed, leak-tested and repainted before dispatch, and cost less than new stock for the same duty.

Head types

  • Open Head: removable lid and clamp ring, for solids, pastes and viscous liquids that need full access.
  • Tight Head: fixed top with bungs, for liquids that must stay sealed in transit.

Typically used for

Solvents, lubricants, paints and coatings, fuels, and hazardous materials.

Plastic Drums (HDPE)

HDPE drums from 30 to 220 litres for chemicals, detergents, water and food concentrates. HDPE does not corrode, which makes it the usual choice for acids, alkalis and anything that would attack a steel wall. Available new or reconditioned.

Head types

  • Open Top: full-diameter opening for powders, pastes and thick liquids.
  • L-Ring / Tight Head: moulded seamless body with bungs, for sealed liquid transport.

Typically used for

Acids, alkalis, detergents, food concentrates, and water.

IBC Tanks — 1000L

1000-litre intermediate bulk containers: an HDPE bottle in a galvanised steel cage on a pallet base, with a discharge valve at the bottom. One IBC replaces roughly five 200-litre drums, and it stacks, so it is the cheaper way to move bulk liquid where a forklift is available.

Specification

  • Capacity: 1000 litres (275 US gallons).
  • Handling: stackable, forklift and pallet-truck accessible from all four sides.

Typically used for

Bulk chemicals, paints and resins, water, and liquid waste collection.

Steel Drum Reconditioning

Used steel drums are washed inside and out, inspected for dents, corrosion and thread damage, leak-tested, and repainted. Bungs, gaskets and clamp rings are replaced where they no longer seal. Returned to you, or bought from you outright.

Plastic Drum Reconditioning

HDPE drums from 30 to 220 litres are hot washed, rinsed and dried, then checked for stress cracking, thread damage and residual odour. Lids, gaskets and caps are replaced as needed so the drum seals as it should.

Covers: HDPE barrels, open top and L-ring drums.

IBC Tank Reconditioning & Rebottling

IBC tanks are washed and pressure-checked. Where the bottle is damaged or has carried something that cannot be washed out, we rebottle: a new HDPE bottle into the existing cage and pallet, which costs less than a new IBC. Cages and valves repaired or replaced.

Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask us most often.

What sizes of drums and IBC tanks do you supply?

HDPE plastic drums from 30 to 220 litres, steel drums in open head and tight head configurations, and IBC tanks at 1000 litres. Tell us the volume you need to move and we will suggest the container that fits it.

Do you deliver outside Sharjah?

Yes. We collect and deliver across all seven emirates — Sharjah, Dubai, Ajman, Abu Dhabi, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. Our yard is in Al Sajaa Industrial Area, Sharjah.

What is the difference between a new and a reconditioned drum?

A reconditioned drum is a used drum that has been washed inside and out, inspected, leak-tested and — for steel — repainted, with bungs, gaskets and clamp rings replaced where needed. It costs less than new and, for most industrial duties, does the same job. New stock is the right choice where the contents or the customer require it.

Do you buy empty drums and IBC tanks?

Yes. If empty steel drums, plastic drums or IBC tanks are accumulating on your site, send a photograph and a rough count on WhatsApp and we will quote for collection.

Can you clean our own drums and return them?

Yes — that is our washing and reconditioning service. We collect from your site, wash and dry the containers, replace closures that no longer seal, and return them ready to refill on an agreed schedule.

What is rebottling an IBC tank?

Rebottling puts a new HDPE bottle into the existing steel cage and pallet base. It is used when the bottle is damaged or has carried something that cannot be washed out, and it costs less than buying a complete new IBC.

Steel drums or plastic drums — which do I need?

Steel suits solvents, lubricants, paints and fuels, and is the stronger container for stacking and rough handling. HDPE suits acids, alkalis, detergents, water and food concentrates, because it does not corrode. If the contents would attack a steel wall, use HDPE.
