Used Stock We Sell

Everything we sell second hand is reconditioned at our own yard before it leaves. The range is the same one we handle new:

  • Used steel drums — open head, with a removable lid and clamp ring; and tight head, with a fixed top and bungs. Steel drums reaching us have typically held solvents, lubricants, paints and coatings, fuels or hazardous materials, and what was in them previously decides how they are prepared.
  • Used plastic drums (HDPE), 30 to 220 litres — open top, and L-ring tight head. Typical previous contents are acids, alkalis, detergents, food concentrates and water. Also called plastic barrels; same item.
  • Used IBC tanks, 1000 litre — an HDPE bottle inside a galvanised steel cage on a pallet base, with a discharge valve. See IBC tanks for the cage, valve and rebottling detail.

Second hand steel barrels and metal drums are repainted where the reconditioning inspection calls for it. Tell us the product going in, the litre size and the closure type you are after, and we will tell you what we have that fits it — and say so plainly where nothing we have does.

What "Used" Means Here — Washed, Reconditioned or Rebottled

"Used" covers more than one thing in this market, and the three are not interchangeable. We separate them plainly:

  • Washed — the container is washed inside and out and dried. Nothing is replaced. We run washing as a collect-wash-return service on containers you already own and intend to keep using: they come to the yard and the same units come back to you.
  • Reconditioned — washed, dried, surface prepared (steel shot blasted or washed depending on what it previously held; HDPE hot washed), inspected for dents, corrosion, thread damage, stress cracking and residual odour, leak tested, with bungs, gaskets, lids, clamp rings and caps replaced where they will not seal, steel repainted where required, then palletised and dispatched. That is what our second hand stock has been through.
  • Rebottled — an IBC term: a new HDPE bottle fitted into your existing galvanised steel cage and pallet base.

The full sequence is set out on drum reconditioning. It is worth asking which of the three you are being quoted for, from us or from anyone else, because the three describe genuinely different amounts of work on the container.

What the Inspection Looks At

Whether a used container comes back into service is decided at inspection, container by container, at the yard — it cannot honestly be settled from a description or a photograph alone. What we can set out is exactly what that inspection covers, so you know what to look at before you set a pallet of empties aside:

  • Dents — how far the steel body is deformed, and whether the deformation reaches the chime or the closure.
  • Corrosion — how deep it has gone, and whether it is confined to the surface that shot blasting takes off.
  • Thread damage — bungs, caps and gaskets are replaced as part of reconditioning where they will not seal; damage to the thread in the container itself is assessed separately.
  • Stress cracking in HDPE — crazing around the L-ring, the handles or the base of a plastic drum.
  • Residual odour — whether a smell survives washing, and what that says about what is left in the container.

Every reconditioned container is leak tested before it goes back out. On IBC tanks, a cage and pallet base can still be sound when the bottle is not, which is exactly the case rebottling exists for. Tell us the previous contents accurately — solvents, fuels and hazardous materials on the steel side, acids, alkalis and food concentrates on the HDPE side all change how a container is handled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sell used drums as well as buy them?

Both. We sell reconditioned steel drums, plastic drums and IBC tanks from the yard at Al Sajaa Industrial Area, Sharjah, and we buy empty steel drums, plastic drums and IBC tanks from companies across the UAE, with collection across all seven emirates.

What is the difference between a washed, a reconditioned and a rebottled container?

Washed means cleaned inside and out and dried, with nothing replaced — we run that as a collect-wash-return service on containers you own and keep. Reconditioned means washed, dried, surface prepared, inspected, leak tested, with bungs, gaskets, lids, clamp rings and caps replaced where they will not seal and steel repainted where required; that is what our second hand stock has been through. Rebottled applies to IBC tanks only: a new HDPE bottle fitted into your existing galvanised steel cage and pallet base.

Which emirates do you collect from?

All seven. Collection and delivery run across the UAE from the yard at Al Sajaa Industrial Area, Sharjah, covering Sharjah, Dubai, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Abu Dhabi, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah.

What decides whether a used drum or IBC tank can be reconditioned?

It is decided at inspection at the yard rather than from a description. The inspection covers dents, corrosion, thread damage, stress cracking in HDPE and residual odour that survives washing, and every reconditioned container is leak tested. On IBC tanks the cage and pallet base can still be sound when the bottle is not, which is what rebottling is for.

Do you need to know what was previously in the containers?

Yes, and it is the most important detail. Previous contents decide the surface preparation — steel is shot blasted or washed depending on what it held, HDPE is hot washed — and they also decide how the container is handled at the yard.

Can I see the containers before buying?

Yes. The yard at Al Sajaa Industrial Area, Opposite Al Asal Marble Yard, Yard No. 27, Sharjah lists hours of Monday to Sunday, 08:00 to 20:00, and you are welcome to inspect stock in person.
